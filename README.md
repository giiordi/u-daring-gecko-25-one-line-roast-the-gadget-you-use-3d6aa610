One original line, at most 200 characters, roasting a gadget I use every
day — judged pairwise by an AI judge on whether it roasts a daily gadget,
is actually funny, feels fresh, is neatly worded, and sounds like one
person's specific gripe.

The line:

> My smartwatch just congratulated me for standing up — apparently sitting
> was the hard part of my day.

A smartwatch that hands out praise for standing up: the joke lands on the
first read, the angle is fresh (a gadget proud of the easiest thing I do),
and the turn at the end flips who did the hard work — me, sitting.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) validates the line against the gate
(1–200 chars, single non-blank line) and writes it to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` with no trailing newline.
